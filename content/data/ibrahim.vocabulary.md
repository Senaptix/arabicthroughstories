## Same root, different form

Acceptable where the form genuinely differs (past vs present, singular vs plural, noun vs verb) — that is how the book teaches conjugation. Listed so it stays a deliberate choice.

- p30 **إِلَّا** (except) ~ p12 أَلَا (do you not? / will you not?)
- p32 **يُفَهِّمَ** (to make understand) ~ p9 يَفْهَمُ (he understands)
- p33 **اعْبُدِ** (worship! (command)) ~ p27 أَعْبُدُ (I worship)
- p35 **حُزْنٌ** (sadness) ~ p14 حَزِنَ (he became sad)
- p39 **إِذًا** (then) ~ p10 إِذَا (when)
- p45 **أَمْرٌ** (command) ~ p24 أَمَرَ (He commanded)
