# Roots and word families

Same principle as Book 1: a back-of-book appendix built entirely from words
the child has already met in the story. Nothing is added to the story pages,
and verb patterns stay out.

Roots are the one insight that turns Arabic vocabulary from memorisation into
pattern recognition, and a child can *see* it — three letters, a family of
words.

⚠️ **IN PROGRESS.** These are the families visible in the pages glossed so
far. Yusuf's richest family is ر أ ي, around the dreams that carry the whole
story, and it cannot be built until the later pages are glossed — every member
of a family must already be in `yusuf.vocabulary.md`.
